Frequently asked questions

Practical answers before you pick a tier.

What does “10 months for 12” mean on annual billing?

You pay the equivalent of ten monthly charges up front and keep the server for twelve months. The per-month figure on annual cards is that total divided by twelve for Growth, Rs 4,800 × 10 ÷ 12 = Rs 4,000/mo.

Do I get root access on every plan?

Yes. Every VPS includes full root or Administrator access over SSH or RDP where licensed. You choose the OS at order time.

Can I pay in PKR with JazzCash or EasyPaisa?

Yes for PKR invoices. Card payments cover PKR, USD and GBP. PayPal is available on USD and GBP invoices. Bank transfer works for PKR (and USD on request).

Is this live PKHosting checkout?

No. This page is an assessment demo with invented VPS prices. It opens offline from index.html and does not call billing APIs.

How do currency and billing choices persist?

When JavaScript is available, billing period and currency are stored in localStorage and restored after a reload. Without JavaScript, monthly PKR prices remain visible in the page source.
